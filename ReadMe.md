## About Me

I am a final-year Electrical and Electronics Engineering student at **Konya Technical University**, graduating in 2027, focused on **embedded systems, robotics, and autonomous platforms**. I work close to the hardware: reading datasheets, bringing up STM32 peripherals, debugging communication buses, integrating sensors with ROS 2, and translating schematics into routed PCBs.

My current work spans **STM32 firmware, FreeRTOS, micro-ROS, PID motor control, sensor interfaces, EKF sensor fusion, ROS 2 Nav2, and mixed hardware/software debugging**. I also have hands-on computer-vision experience from a TEKNOFEST finalist underwater robotics project, where I developed YOLOv8-based target detection and cable-following software.

**Open to:** embedded firmware, robotics, electronics design, autonomous systems, and graduate/junior engineering opportunities.
